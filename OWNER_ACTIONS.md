# Owner actions

Only external decisions or information needed from the owner are listed here.

- **Hosting and deployment authorization:** choose/confirm a Node 24+ Canadian host with persistent storage; authorize deployment and DNS changes when ready. Supply domain access through the host's secure workflow.
- **Verified contact number:** verify the intended business number before enabling `CONTACT_PHONE_VERIFIED`. The default `(833) 696-9996` is configuration-only and is not represented as verified.
- **Inquiry routing:** supply an approved notification endpoint/token and destination, or designate a person to operate the secure inbox. Confirm any processor's data residency and processing terms.
- **Business/privacy particulars:** provide the legal operating entity, business address and privacy contact; approve policies against actual operations and retention, with qualified review as appropriate.
- **Providers and directory:** supply verified provider relationships, service areas, current course approvals and source evidence before naming schools or offering confirmed availability. Disclose any referral compensation arrangement.
- **Search Console:** provide domain verification access/token after hosting is approved.

No payment processing, government accreditation, partner network or nationwide physical offices have been assumed.
